Rules for Spellbound

The Librarian's Word is Law: The Librarian is impartial. If you have a problem or want to do something unusual, see the Librarian. The Librarian's power is absolute - and cannot be affected by abilities!

Winning and Losing: You can achieve most of your goals simply by talking to people. The Librarian will announce when the game is over. If you haven't succeeded by that point - you're too late! Be warned - not everyone here will want you to succeed!

Doing Stuff: Ordinary actions are resolved by simply carrying them out. If you want to try something unusual (such as set fire to a classroom), see the Librarian. The Librarian knows everything - and will be able to tell you the outcome of whatever it is that you are trying to do. (For example, there may be magical fireproofing.) Do use your imagination, though! - this is a very flexible game, and you can do all sorts of things beyond what's listed in these rules.

Fighting: Tremaine Castle can be a dangerous place! Your character may wish to grapple with, come to blows with, stab or even cast a spell on one of the other characters. Should you with to do something along these lines though, don't just dive in! See the Librarian first and tell them what you plan to do so they can oversee and give you more detailed rules if it's necessary.

Leaving the Castle: The police have surrounded the castle, and no-one will be allowed to leave until the criminal is found and arrested, or the end of the school day. Anyone attempting to leave will be arrested, no questions asked.

Item Cards: Any items of importance within the game are represented as items or item cards - and the only items that can affect the game are those detailed on the cards or item. If you do not have an item or item card, you do not have that item with you.

Time: Spellbound is played over three hours.
