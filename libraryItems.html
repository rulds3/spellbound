Library Items

Amulet of Power

Whoever brings you both halves of the amulet. Once one ability is used, the other cannot be used by the same person. But if it passes to a new owner, both abilities are available again. Each person can only ever use one ability.

Bomb

  • Give it to the person who brings you:
    • Plastic Explosives
    • Spark Plug
    • Digital Timer
  • Must plant before 8:45 in plain sight, but slightly hidden.
  • If someone finds it, play rock-paper-scissors:
    • If they win: they get the bomb and may keep it diffused or re-plant it.
    • If they lose or tie: they must wait 10 minutes before trying again.

Letter from Marvin Bennet

  • Can submit prescriptions every 20 minutes.
  • Needs:
    • Moonbeam Dust
    • Powdered Gargoyle Claws
    • Shredded Mandrake Leaves
  • Tell Lee Roderick the results after 20 minutes.
    • 0 correct: Make up side effect.
    • 1 correct: Remembers name.
    • 2 correct: Remembers childhood memory.
    • 3 correct: Memories restored, deliver letter.

Popularity Status Card

Give to whoever "drinks" the purple/gold potion.

Scene of the Crime

Keep it, but let anyone see it who asks. If no one asks by 7:30, suggest it to the detectives.
  • First person who asks - also give them the Scrap of paper to keep.
  • Second person - Silver Amulet
  • Third person - Defensive Magic Lesson Plan book

Sealed Red Box

How to destroy or seal the gargoyle stone
  • Potion to Seal or Destroy the Gargoyle Stone: Powdered Dragon Fang, Moonbeam Dust, Lizard Scales, Ashes. Put the Gargoyle stone in the potion.
  • To Destroy it, Angela McIntosh and four other wizards must touch their wands to the potion and say the incantation: “Attero Mallum!” If the gargoyle is successfully destroyed, then take the ingredient item cards and the gargoyle stone. Congratulate the players on destroying the stone.
  • To Seal it, they must have something to seal the Gargoyle Stone into – like the red box. Then, after creating the potion, any five wizards must dip their wands into the potion and say the incantation “Arceo Mallum!” Exchange the ingredients, red box, and gargoyle stone for the Sealed Red Box Item card.
If the gargoyle stone is sealed into the red box, and then the box is opened, take the Sealed red box item card and give the player the Gargoyle stone item card and the Empty red box item card.
