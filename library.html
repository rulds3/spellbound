The Library

Library Instructions

Amulet of Power - Whoever brings you both halves of the amulet.

Once one ability is used, the other cannot be used by the same person. But if it passes to a new owner, both abilities are available again. Each person can only ever use one ability.

Bomb

  • Give it to the person who brings you:
    • Plastic Explosives
    • Spark Plug
    • Digital Timer
  • Must plant before 8:45 in plain sight, but slightly hidden.
  • If someone finds it, play rock-paper-scissors:
  • If they win: they get the bomb and may keep it diffused or re-plant it.
  • If they lose or tie: they must wait 10 minutes before trying again.

Letter from Marvin Bennet

  • Can submit prescriptions every 20 minutes.
  • Needs:
    • Moonbeam Dust
    • Powdered Gargoyle Claws
    • Shredded Mandrake Leaves
  • Tell Lee Roderick the results after 20 minutes.

Library Rules

  • Players may not talk in the library.
  • Players may not remove books from the library.

If a player asks:

  • For a specific book: Give it to them right away.
  • About a topic: Wait a minute while you "look."
  • About an unlisted topic: Say you couldn't find anything, but show them a different unseen book.
  • Is milling around: Show them something that hasn't been seen yet.

Scene of the Crime

  • Keep it available for anyone who asks.
  • First person who asks gets the scrap of paper.
  • Second person gets the Silver Amulet.
  • Third person gets the Defensive Magic Lesson Plan book.
