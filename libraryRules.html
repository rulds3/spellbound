Game Rules

Fighting –

To attack, must have a weapon OR Magical Attack ability AND wand.

In rock, paper, scissors...if attacker:

  • Wins: The victim falls unconscious.
  • Ties: No effect.
  • Loses: The victim can attack back or flee and can’t be attacked by the same person for 10 min.

Unconscious Characters –

  • They have to be still (unconscious) for 10 min or until healed.
  • If it’s after the End of Exams announcement and they aren’t healed, they die. Use the With Your Last Dying Breath card.
  • An unconscious character can’t be killed...finished off.
  • They can be searched...librarian offers all items to searcher.

Pickpocket –

  • They can only steal 1 gold coin, no more.
  • If the victim doesn’t have the requested item, take something else at random.
  • If the victim doesn’t have anything, the pickpocket attempt fails.

Poison –

  • Only healed with a healing potion.
  • Victim temporarily loses their abilities.
  • Healed after 10 min if before final exams.
  • Dead after 10 min if after final exams. Use With Your Last Dying Breath card.

Arrests –

Can only be done after End of Exams Announcement.

  • Librarian keeps arrest warrants once used.
  • Librarian and detective both take them to jail.
  • The arresting detective may confiscate any wands, weapons, or items that they suspect to be related to a crime, but no coins, first aid items, or other valuables or personal effects.
  • The person under arrest can’t use physical abilities like pickpocketing, attacking, etc., but can use talking abilities.
  • The person can resist arrest by attacking the detective, or using Fio Bestia, Velieris, or Glacialis.

Escape is possible if:

  • Someone can cut the ropes.
  • The jailed player comes up with a good idea and wins rock, paper, scissors with the librarian.
  • They use the Amulet of Power.
  • A detective frees them.

Capturing (Rope) –

  • Rock, paper, scissors with victim.
  • If unsuccessful, must wait 10 min before trying again.
  • Escaping is the same as arresting.
  • If captured before the End of Exams announcement, they automatically find an easy way to escape.

Red Paint –

If someone chooses to paint another red X, have them leave the playing area for a few moments, then:

“Oh, no! A red X has been painted on _________ door!”

Ventus Contego – Shantell Andersen and Skyler Patterson

  • They can’t attack or be attacked for 10 minutes.

Reverse Alchemy – Lee Roderick

  • Can’t be used on popularity potion.

Levitas – Veronica Olsen

  • If Veronica wins rock, paper, scissors with librarian, victim is unconscious for 10 minutes (see unconscious).
  • If successful the first time, can’t use a 2nd time on the same player.

End of the Game

After all Game End announcements:

  • Read “The Solution”.
  • Have everyone point to (but don’t reveal yet):
    • Who they think has been stealing wands.
    • Who they think is Callie Fu.
    • The person who ruined Casey’s motorcycle.
    • The person trying to blow up the school.
  • Have everyone say who they were, what they were up to, and whether they succeeded or not.
